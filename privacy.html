DragonRunners Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026
Data controller: AMZ STUDIO
Privacy contact: dragonrunners.soporte@gmail.com

1. What DragonRunners is

DragonRunners is a free virtual reality game for Meta Quest in which you play as a dragon, alone or online with other people. This policy explains what data is processed when you play, what for, how long it is kept, who it is shared with and what rights you have.

In short:

  • No advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking tools in the game.
  • We do not sell your data.
  • Your voice is not recorded. It is sent in real time to nearby players and is not stored.
  • Error reports are optional and off by default.

2. Minimum age

The game is intended for people aged 13 or over. Meta does not allow children under 10 to use apps from the Meta Horizon Store, and accounts for ages 10 to 12 are managed by parents or guardians under Meta's social restrictions. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided data (for example, a name that appears in a report), write to dragonrunners.soporte@gmail.com and we will delete it.

5. Who we share data with

  • Other players in your room see your display name, your dragon's look and position, and hear your voice if you are nearby and they have not muted you.
  • Meta Platforms (Meta Horizon): your account, license and purchases are handled through Meta, which processes its data under its own privacy policy. To check your account and your payments, our server queries Meta (graph.oculus.com), sending your Meta user ID and the one-time identity code, and the user ID to read which packs you have paid for and have not been credited yet. We do not send Meta your reports or your voice.
  • Meta's reporting service: if you use the "Report" button in the Meta menu inside the game, the report is collected by Meta's reporting service and reaches us by email (the reason chosen and the data Meta includes about the reported player, such as their display name). We use it only to review the reported conduct and keep it like the other reports (90 days).
  • Server provider: the game servers are hosted by DigitalOcean, in the United States. It acts as a data processor and accesses data only to provide hosting.
  • Authorities: only if a law or a valid court order requires us to.

We do not sell personal data, we do not use it for advertising and we do not pass it to analytics or marketing companies.

6. International transfers

If your country is different from the one where the server is hosted, your connection data will be transferred to that country. The server is located in the United States, so your connection and match data are processed there.

7. Security

Connections to the game server are validated on the server (for example impossible positions, report frequency and report size). Only the people in charge of moderation have access to reports and bans. No system is perfect: if we detect a breach that affects you, we will tell you as the law requires.

8. Your rights and how to use them

You can ask us, free of charge, at any time, to:

  • Access: tell you whether we process data about you and which.
  • Rectify: correct inaccurate data.
  • Delete: erase your data (for example reports or bans that mention you).
  • Object or restrict: stop processing it in certain cases.
  • Port: give you your data in a common format.
  • Withdraw consent: you can turn off error reports in Settings and revoke the microphone permission in your Quest headset settings at any time.

How to request deletion of your data

  1. Send an email to dragonrunners.soporte@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my data”.
  2. Write your Meta username and the approximate date you played. To delete your Scales wallet and purchases, add the 10-character support code shown on the Wardrobe board in the game.
  3. We delete your data and confirm by email within 30 days (usually much sooner). It is free.

The same steps, on their own page: Data deletion instructions.

The rest of your rights

To use any of the rights above, write to dragonrunners.soporte@gmail.com with your Meta username and the approximate date you played. For anything about your Scales or purchases, add the 10-character support code shown on the Wardrobe board in the game: that lets us find your wallet without knowing your name. We will answer within 30 days. We never charge for handling these requests. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the data protection authority of your country.

What we do with your request: Deletion: we delete everything that mentions you from the reports in which you appear as the reported player, from bans and from error reports linked to your username or connection; if you appear as the person who made a report, we remove your name from it and keep the report (it is about someone else). Your Scales wallet, with its support code, is deleted entirely, also from the internal backup copies and the movement log (you lose the balance and what you bought). Access and portability: we send you a JSON file with what we keep about you. We only keep your request as a note with no personal data (date, type and counts).

Residents of California and other US states: we do not sell or share your personal information for targeted advertising. You can exercise your access and deletion rights using the same email address.

Deleting your data in your Meta account: you manage your Meta account data from your Meta account settings.

9. Headset permissions

The game asks for microphone permission (only for voice between players) and internet access (to play online). You can deny the microphone: the game keeps working without voice. We do not ask for camera, location, contacts or storage.

10. Data the game does not collect

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or recordings of your room, biometric data, eye or face tracking, or health data. The game uses your head and controller positions only to move your dragon in real time.

11. Changes to this policy

If we make a significant change to this policy, we will update the date above and notify you in the game or on the store listing before the change takes effect.
