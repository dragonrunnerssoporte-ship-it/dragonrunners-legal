DragonRunners Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026
App: DragonRunners (virtual reality game for Meta Quest, published on the Meta Horizon Store)
Data controller: AMZ STUDIO (United States)
Privacy contact and data deletion requests: dragonrunners.soporte@gmail.com

1. Summary

This policy applies only to the DragonRunners app. In short:

  • We collect only the data needed to run the game. This includes your Meta user ID, your Meta display name (user profile), your in-app purchase records, a one-time identity code, your IP address, your head and controller position, your voice (live only, if you allow the microphone), your Scales wallet, optional error reports and, if you report someone or are reported, moderation reports. Section 2 lists every item.
  • Your voice is never recorded or stored. It is relayed live to nearby players only.
  • No advertising, no third-party analytics, no tracking, and we never sell your data.
  • Any user, anywhere, can ask us to delete their data at any time, for free and without giving a reason (section 7).

2. Data we collect

DataWhat it is

2.3 Data created while you play

DataWhat it is
IP address and connection data (your IP address, the port and a connection number our server assigns to you, and the times you connect and disconnect)Unavoidable when connecting to our game server.
Installation IDA random identifier the game creates the first time it runs (it is not your name, account or headset serial). It is sent to our server when you connect and is not stored on our server.
Live game dataYour dragon's look, position and match state.
Scales walletYour Scales balance, cosmetics bought with Scales, the identifiers of the Scales packs you bought (so they are never credited twice), date of your last free gift, the Scales you earned from finished online rounds today and the (UTC) date of that day (stored as round_today and round_day, used for the daily limit of 50 Scales per day for rounds), and a log of wallet movements (gifts, purchases, round rewards, refunds and support adjustments, each line with the date, the amount, the balance and your short support code). It is linked to a one-way hash (digest) of your Meta user ID, never to the ID itself. The hash cannot be turned back into your ID, but we still treat it as your personal data: it is deleted when you ask (section 7).
Moderation reports and bansIf someone reports you or you report someone: date, room, display names and connection numbers, the reported player's IP address and an unrecognizable digest of their Meta account, and the reason chosen from a fixed list (harassment, offensive language, cheating, other).

Daily server backupsOnce a day the server saves a copy of its data files for recovery after a failure. The copy contains the same data as the files themselves (Scales wallets, moderation reports and bans, error reports) and nothing else.
Server activity logThe messages the game server writes while it runs (for example that it started, how many rooms are open, or an error). It does not contain IP addresses, display names, Meta user IDs or purchase identifiers; only counts and connection numbers.

5. Who we share data with

  • Other players in your room see your display name, your dragon's look and position, and hear your voice if they are nearby and have not muted you.
  • Meta Platforms: our server sends your Meta user ID and the one-time identity code to Meta (graph.oculus.com) to verify your account and your purchases, as described in section 3. If you use the "Report" option in the Meta menu, Meta's reporting service sends us the report by email; we use it only to review that behavior and keep it like other reports (90 days).
  • DigitalOcean (our server hosting provider, in the United States) stores and processes the server data only to host the game, on our behalf.
  • Authorities, only if a valid law or court order requires it.

We do not sell, rent or share your data with advertisers, analytics companies or data brokers.

6. Where data is processed

Our game server is hosted in the United States. If you play from another country, your connection and game data are transferred to and processed in the United States.

7. Requesting data deletion (all users)

Every user, wherever they live and whatever their age, can ask us to delete their data at any time. It is free, you do not need to give a reason and we do not need to approve it.

  1. Send an email to dragonrunners.soporte@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data".
  2. Tell us your Meta username or the display name you use in the game, so we can find the reports and bans that mention you. Error reports contain no name: we locate them through the connection recorded in any report about you or, if there is none, through the IP address you played from, if you choose to give it to us; in any case they are deleted automatically after 30 days at most. To delete your Scales wallet as well, send us one of these two things (whichever you can see): your Meta ID, shown at the bottom right of the Wardrobe board in the game as "Meta ID: …", or the 10-character support code shown at the bottom left of the same board when you are online. The wallet is stored only under an unrecognizable digest, so our server turns the Meta ID or the code into that digest to find it; it does not store the number you send. If you can see neither, or you no longer have the game, just tell us: we will help you find your wallet and delete it.
  3. We delete your data and confirm it by email within 30 days (usually much sooner). The deletion is applied to the live server data at once; the copies of that data in the daily backups disappear automatically within 7 more days at most, and we tell you so in the confirmation.

What we delete: your Scales wallet (including its round counters, its internal copies and its movement log), the reports and bans in which you appear as the reported player, and error reports linked to you. If you made a report about someone else, we remove your name and connection number from it and keep the rest of the report, because it describes another player's conduct and no longer contains any data about you. Deleting your wallet also deletes the Scales and cosmetics in it. The daily server backups are not edited: they keep a copy of your data until their 7 days pass, and then it is deleted automatically, so your data is gone from all our systems at most 7 days after we carry out your request. We keep no other data after a deletion request (only a log line with the date, the type of request and the number of items deleted, with no personal data), and there is no legal reason that would stop us from fulfilling one.

If you delete your Meta account, we no longer receive anything about you; the data we still hold expires automatically (section 4) or is deleted on request as described here.

The same instructions are on their own page: Data deletion instructions.

You can also delete the data stored on your headset yourself by uninstalling the game, and manage your Meta account data from your Meta account settings.

8. Your other rights

Every user, wherever they live, can also ask us at any time, free of charge, to:

  • Access the data we keep about them, or receive a copy in a common format (JSON).
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Object to or restrict some processing.
  • Withdraw consent: turn off error reports in Settings and revoke the microphone permission in the Quest headset settings at any time.

Write to dragonrunners.soporte@gmail.com and we will answer within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Children

DragonRunners is intended for people aged 13 or older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us data, write to dragonrunners.soporte@gmail.com and we will delete it. This does not limit section 7: every user of any age can request deletion.

10. Security

The game server validates everything players send (for example, impossible positions and too many reports). Only the people in charge of moderation can see reports and bans, and the server backups can only be read by the person who administers the server. Account IDs are stored only as one-way hashes. If a security breach affects your data, we will notify you and the competent authority without undue delay. To report a security vulnerability in the game or the server, write to dragonrunners.soporte@gmail.com with the subject "Security".

11. Headset permissions

The game asks for the microphone (only for voice chat between players) and internet access (to play online). You can deny the microphone and the game keeps working without voice. We do not ask for the camera, location, contacts or storage.

12. Data we do not collect

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, recordings of your room, biometric data, eye or face tracking, or health data.

13. Changes to this policy

If we make a significant change, we will update the date at the top and tell players in the game or on the store listing before it takes effect.
