DragonRunners — Data deletion instructions

Last updated: October 6, 2026
Developer: AMZ STUDIO

You can ask us to delete the data DragonRunners keeps about you at any time. It is free and you do not need to give a reason.

How to request it

  1. Send an email to dragonrunners.soporte@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my data”.
  2. In the email write:
  3. Your Meta username (or the display name you use in the game).
  4. The approximate date you played.
  5. Only if you want your Scales wallet and purchases deleted: the 10-character support code shown on the Wardrobe board inside the game.
  6. We delete your data and confirm by email within 30 days (usually much sooner).

What we delete

  • Reports and bans that mention you as the reported player, and error reports linked to your username or connection.
  • If you appear as the person who made a report, we remove your name from it and keep the report (it is about someone else).
  • Your Scales wallet (balance, cosmetics bought with Scales, last gift date and movement log), also from the server's internal backup copies. You lose the balance and what you bought.
  • If you ask for it, we first send you a file with the data we keep about you (access and portability).

What we do not have

We never store your voice, your location, your contacts or recordings of your room. Settings, your chosen dragon and the local copy of your balance live only on your headset: they are deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

What Meta keeps

Your Meta account, the license of the app and the record of your payments are managed by Meta under its own privacy policy. To delete those, use the settings of your Meta account.

Automatic deletion

Even without a request, data is deleted by itself: reports after 90 days, error reports after 30 days, bans when they expire, and Scales wallets with no activity for 24 months. See the full list in the Privacy Policy.
