DragonRunners — Data deletion instructions

Last updated: October 9, 2026
Developer: AMZ STUDIO

Every user, wherever they live and whatever their age, can ask us to delete the data DragonRunners keeps about them at any time. It is free, you do not need to give a reason and we do not need to approve it. There is no legal reason that would stop us from fulfilling a request.

How to request it

  1. Send an email to dragonrunners.soporte@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my data”.
  2. In the email, write your Meta username (or the display name you use in the game), so we can find your data. To delete your Scales wallet as well, send us one of these two things (whichever you can see): your Meta ID, shown at the bottom right of the Wardrobe board inside the game as “Meta ID: …”, or the 10-character support code shown at the bottom left of the same board when you are online. The wallet is stored only under a one-way hash, so our server turns the Meta ID or the code into that hash to find it; it does not store the number you send. If you can see neither, or you no longer have the game, just tell us: we will help you find your wallet and delete it.
  3. We delete your data and confirm by email within 30 days (usually much sooner). The deletion is applied to the live server data at once; the copies of that data in the daily backups disappear automatically within 7 more days at most, and we tell you so in the confirmation.

What we delete

  • Reports and bans that mention you as the reported player, and error reports linked to your username or connection.
  • If you appear as the person who made a report, we remove your name and connection number from it and keep the rest of the report, because it describes another player's conduct and no longer contains any data about you.
  • Your Scales wallet (balance, cosmetics bought with Scales, last gift date, today's round-reward counters and movement log), also from the server's internal copies of the wallet file. You lose the balance and what you bought.
  • If you ask for it, we first send you a file with the data we keep about you (access and portability).

Backups

Once a day the server saves a backup copy of its data files, kept for 7 days and then deleted automatically. Backups are not edited, so a copy of your data stays in them until those 7 days pass: your data is gone from all our systems at most 7 days after we carry out your request. We keep nothing else after a request except a log line with the date, the type of request and the number of items deleted, with no personal data.

What we do not have

We never store your voice, your location, your contacts or recordings of your room. Settings, your chosen dragon and the local copy of your balance live only on your headset: they are deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

What Meta keeps

Your Meta account, the license of the app and the record of your payments are managed by Meta under its own privacy policy. To delete those, use the settings of your Meta account.

Automatic deletion

Even without a request, data is deleted by itself: reports after 90 days, error reports after 30 days, bans when they expire, Scales wallets with no activity for 24 months, server backups after 7 days and the server activity log after 90 days. See the full list in the Privacy Policy.
